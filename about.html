About

A second look at the food on Ottawa’s plates.

Ottawa Plated is an ongoing visual collection of beautiful food made in Ottawa. From neighbourhood bakeries to ambitious tasting menus, we celebrate the dishes that deserve a second look.

There are no rankings, reviews or scores. A sandwich can hang beside a tasting-menu course. The only criterion is that the food is worth looking at.

Every photograph is published with the permission of the restaurant or photographer, and credited. To suggest a dish, submit it here.
